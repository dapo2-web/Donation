Our Story

Where It All Began

My siblings Hopey and Charlie were born with Down syndrome. Growing up alongside them didn't just shape who I am — it completely redefined how I see the world.

I watched them laugh louder, love deeper, and push through challenges that would make most people give up. They never asked for pity. They asked for presence, for understanding, for a world that would simply meet them where they are.

That's why I built this. Not out of sadness, but out of pure admiration. Hopey and Charlie made me want to fight — for awareness, for resources, for families who are walking the same beautiful, challenging road we walked.

Every dollar donated goes directly toward supporting families, funding research, and building a community where people with Down syndrome can thrive with dignity.
